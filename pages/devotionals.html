FLC Advent Devotional

Four weekly reflections exploring Hope, Love, Peace, and Joy through Scripture, prayer, and Christ's transformative incarnation.

by Jarrel Oliveira

How to Use This Devotional

1

Set Aside Time Daily

Choose a consistent time each day for your devotional reading—morning, lunch, or evening.

2

Read the Scripture

Start with the daily scripture passage and let God's Word speak to your heart.

3

Reflect & Journal

Use the reflection questions and journal prompts to deepen your understanding.

4

Apply & Pray

End with the daily prayer and consider how to apply what you've learned.

Weekly Devotionals

Week 1 - Hope

Nov 30-Dec 6

Scripture: Titus 1:1-3

God's plan for hope began before time itself. In Christ, hope is embodied and the promise of eternal life is fulfilled.

Week 2 - Love

Dec 7-Dec 13

Scripture: John 3:16

God's love is both incomprehensible and deeply personal. Advent invites us to receive this love with humility.

Week 3 - Peace

Dec 14-Dec 20

Scripture: Isaiah 9:6

Christ, the Prince of Peace, restores what was broken by sin and transforms the chaos of our hearts.

Week 4 - Joy

Dec 21-Dec 28

Scripture: Luke 2:8-11

The good news of Christ's birth brings deep, enduring gladness rooted in God's faithfulness.
